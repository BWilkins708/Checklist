    How to use Checklist

    Items

    • Add – type in the box and press Enter or +.
    • Tick off – tap the circle. The bar at the top shows how much is done.
    • Edit – double-tap the text or tap ✎. Enter saves, Escape cancels.
    • Remove – swipe the item left, or tap ✕. Clear checked items removes every ticked one.
    • Reorder – drag the ⠿ handle up or down.
    • Oops? – after removing anything, tap Undo in the message at the bottom.

    Lists

    • Switch – tap a tab at the top. Each tab shows how many are done, like 2/5. Swipe the tabs sideways if there are lots.
    • New list – tap + New list, then type its name.
    • Rename – tap the list's title.
    • Reorder lists – press and hold a tab, then drag it.
    • Delete – tap Delete list at the bottom.

    Buttons at the top

    • Share (the box with an arrow) – send the list by text, email and more.
    • ⋯ – untick everything to reuse a list, duplicate a list, or back up and restore all your lists.

    Good to know

    • Everything saves by itself on this device, and works without internet. The top of each list shows when it was last changed.
    • Lists aren't shared between devices or browsers. Use ⋯ → Back up all lists to keep a copy or move them to another device.
    • On iPhone, deleting the app from the home screen deletes its lists, so back up first.
    • Add it to your home screen to use it like an app: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
